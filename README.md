## Brand & Theme Design System
- **Theme:** Warm Royal Heritage Bistro & Burnished Gold
- **Palette:** Onyx Slate (`#0c0d12`), Surface Charcoal (`#131620`), Burnished Gold (`#c59b27`), Warm Amber (`#dfb13c`), Crimson/Veg accent tags.
- **Typography:** Classic Serif headings (`Georgia`, serif) paired with clean geometric UI sans-serif.
- **Geometry:** Crisp rectangular buttons with 4px to 6px corners (zero pill buttons, zero purple gradients).
- **Logo:** Bespoke SVG Royal Archway & Cloche Crest (`public/images/logo/dhunki_logo.svg`).

## Multi-Page Architecture
The website consists of 9 distinct pages:
1. `index.html`: Home page featuring hero presentation, chef specialties, ambiance teaser, and quick navigation.
2. `about.html`: Heritage story, chef philosophy, dining room atmosphere, and family restaurant values.
3. `menu.html`: Full categorized interactive menu with live search, diet badges, and high-resolution scanned official menu cards.
4. `gallery.html`: Filterable high-definition photograph gallery (ambiance, signature cuisine, banquet setup, official tariffs) with lightbox view.
5. `banquet.html`: Banquet & celebration hall bookings for birthdays, ring ceremonies, corporate meetings, and family gatherings.
6. `reservation.html`: Table booking engine with party size, time slots, special dining notes, and WhatsApp confirmation dispatch.
7. `contact.html`: Location directions, operating hours, direct phone numbers (`6207858727`, `9123401594`), and Google Maps route.
8. `privacy.html`: Comprehensive privacy policy detailing customer data protection and booking communication.
9. `terms.html`: Terms & conditions for dining, table holding times, banquet booking policies, and delivery.

## Specialties & Menu Highlights
- **Handi Mutton:** Earthen clay-pot slow braised goat meat with whole rustic spices.
- **Dehati Chicken:** Countryside style chicken cooked in cold-pressed mustard oil with whole roasted garlic.
- **Charcoal Tandoor:** Reshmi Tikka, Chicken Tikka, Tandoori Spring Chicken, and Paneer Tikka.
- **Dum Biryani:** Long-grain aged basmati dum-cooked with chicken or mutton.
- **Tandoori Breads:** Garlic Naan, Lachha Paratha, Masala Kulcha, and Tandoori Roti.
- **Quick Bites & Beverages:** Kurkure Momos, Rolls, Pizzas, Virgin Mojito, and Cold Coffee.
