Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026. Your privacy and trust are of paramount importance to Dhunki Restaurant.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Dhunki Restaurant ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate the restaurant and banquet hall situated at Main Road Rajdhanwar, Barjo, Near Indian Oil Petrol Pump, Giridih 825412, Jharkhand, India. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards concerning the collection, storage, and handling of personal information provided by visitors and dining patrons through this website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal details that you voluntarily submit when reserving dining tables, booking our banquet hall, or contacting our customer service desk:

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone number, mobile number, and email address.
  • Reservation Specifications: Date and time of reservation, number of guests, seating preferences, and dietary requests.
  • Banquet & Event Details: Occasion type (Birthday, Ring Ceremony, Meeting), estimated attendees, and customized catering instructions.

3. Purpose of Processing Your Information

All submitted details are utilized solely for direct customer service operations:

  • To confirm, manage, and verify table dining reservations and banquet event bookings.
  • To dispatch reservation updates or status messages via telephone calls, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • To coordinate doorstep meal delivery within our 5 km service perimeter.
  • To address patron inquiries and continuously elevate dining quality.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We maintain a strict policy against selling, renting, trading, or sharing your personal information with external marketers, advertising brokers, or unauthorized third parties. Information is accessed solely by authorized restaurant management and floor staff strictly for fulfilling your dining requirements.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard organizational and technological safeguards to protect personal data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Data Retention and Customer Rights

You have the right to request the deletion or correction of your contact records from our reservation logs at any time. To request removal or review of your contact details, please contact our management team directly via phone at +91 6207858727 or visit us at our restaurant premises.

7. Updates to This Policy
