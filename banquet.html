Celebration Hall Features

Memorable Gatherings in Rajdhanwar

Dhunki Restaurant offers a dedicated air-conditioned banquet hall designed to comfortably host celebrations, corporate meetings, and family milestones. Our in-house culinary brigade handles everything from starter canapes and live tandoor stations to decadent multi-course buffets.
